# E-commerce Revenue & Orders Performance Analysis
## Summary
This project analyzes revenue and order performance of Brazilian e-commerce transaction data. An ETL pipeline was built using Python, SQL was used to perform analysis, and a Power BI dashboard was built to track product category, seller, and monthly order and revenue performance.

Data Source - https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce

## Tools
- SQL (PostgreSQL)
- Python (pandas)
- Power BI 
- Jupyter Notebook 
- VS Code

## Key Findings:
- Revenue (609.72% increase) and Orders (714.00% increase) both trended upward from January 2017 and August 2018
- Revenue peaks in late 2017 and stabilizes in 2018
- Orders started trending down on November 2017, falling by 13.67% (1031) in 9 months 
- At \$1.25M Beleza Saude products had the highest Revenue(US$) and accounted for 9.29% of Revenue with 9500+ orders
- Cama Mesa Banho products sell high volume with 11,000+ orders and accounted for 7.64% of Revenue (Ranks 3rd in Product Category Revenue) at $1.04M
- Relogios Presentes products can be classified as high income products with about 5700 orders and accounted for 8.71% of Revenue (Ranks 2nd in Product Category Revenue) at $1.18M
- Across all top 10 Product Category, Revenue(US$) ranged between $483,946.60 to $1,2585,91.44  
- High delivery rate (~97%) indicates strong operations
- Total Revenue(US$) is approximately $13.55M and the top 3 states that contribute the most revenue are
    - Sau Paulo(SP) - 38.31% ($5,191,829.48)
    - Rio de Janeiro(RJ) - 13.39% ($1,815,534.97)
    - Minas Gerais(MG) - 11.66% ($1,580,228.93)
